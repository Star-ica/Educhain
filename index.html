Why EduChain

Trust without phone calls and paperwork.

🏫

Approved issuers only

Only institutions approved by the network admin can issue certificates.

🔒

Tamper-proof

Records are immutable and permanently timestamped on BOT Chain.

🪪

Student-bound

Certificates are tied to the student’s wallet and can’t be transferred or sold.

⚡

Instant verification

Anyone can check a certificate by its ID, without connecting a wallet.

🚫

Revocable

Issuers can revoke a fraudulent or mistaken certificate, and verifiers see it immediately.

🔗

Shareable links

Students copy a verification link to include in a CV or job application.

How it works

Three steps from graduation to verification.

1

Institution issues

After graduation, the institution issues the certificate to the student’s wallet.

2

Student shares

The student copies the certificate’s verification link.

3

Employer verifies

The employer opens the link and sees the status, issuer and date.
